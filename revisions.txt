1.0.0: Switch LCB firmware welcome message to semantic Langmuir firmware version.
3.0.0: Replaced DryRun command with DROn and DROff explicit commands. Added two required load parameters: HCDC to disable height control during the cut move, and HCUL to cap upward Z travel above the touch-off position. Both must be sent at the end of the parameter load, after CS, or the load will error
4.0.0: Fixed mid-run Hold stalls by making the hold/release helpers the single owner of the WAIT and START lines, corrected the pierce start position with a time-based enable check, and fixed the stuck retract and <Pause> handling. Loaded cut/pierce/lift heights are now re-applied against the fresh calibration table when CHS homing finishes, and setLiftHeight() stores liftHeight so it reports correctly. The CHS ($HHC) and AF ($HAF) homing routines can now run at the same time, with laserState derived from whichever axes are busy; Cancel$HAF now stops TimerTwo, covers AF_UP_SEC, and clears AF_homed
